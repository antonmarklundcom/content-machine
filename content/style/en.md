# Style guide — English (on camera)

Written to be *said*, by Anton, from a teleprompter. Read every line out loud.
If you run out of breath or stumble, the line is too long.

## Rhythm
- One idea per line. One line is one sentence, usually 6–14 words.
- Short sentence. Then a shorter one. Then a longer one that pays it off.
- Put the new information at the end of the sentence, where the voice lands.
- No line starts with "So," "Now," or "Basically."

## Voice
- Plain, direct, a little dry. A friend who did the paperwork already.
- "You" for the viewer. "I" for Anton's own experience — only if it is true.
- Concrete over general: "45 days" beats "a few weeks"; "the police
  certificate" beats "some documents".
- Confident, not salesy. No "amazing", "game-changer", "insane".

## Cut these
- "In today's video…", "Without further ado", "Let's dive in", "Make sure to
  like and subscribe" (the CTA does its own job, once, at the end).
- Hedging stacks: "it might possibly be that" → "it can".
- Repeating the title back as the first line.

## Numbers and facts
- Say numbers the way they are spoken: "forty-five days", "about two hundred
  dollars". Put the digits in on-screen text instead.
- Every fact has a source. Laws, fees, deadlines and residency rules change —
  they are flagged "verify before recording".
- Never round a legal figure to make it sound better.

## Examples
Bad:  "In this video we're going to be taking a look at the residency process,
      which can be quite long and complicated for a lot of people."
Good: "Everyone still says ninety days.
      That number changed.
      Here's the real one."

Bad:  "There are several documents that you will need to gather."
Good: "You need four documents.
      Most people forget the second one."

## Hooks that fit this channel
- Contradict a number people repeat.
- Name the mistake before the viewer makes it.
- Show the end result first, then how.

## Calls to action
One action, specific, doable today: "Check the expiry date on your police
certificate before you book anything."
