## Decisions

- Existing facts are marked `verified` and get their brand's language in the migration; hand-made
  facts (`createFact`) are verified too — only imported unchecked facts are not (§1.48). No behaviour change.
- Extra columns beyond §2, since lanes 2/3 can never add one: `posts.external_container_id`,
  `last_publish_attempt_at`, `notes` (O13 reel polling + backoff), `assets.updated_at`,
  `social_competitors.followers`/`last_error`/`created_at`, `brand_families.created_at`.
- `posts.body`/`caption` nullable (a post in `idea` has no draft yet); `sha256` is `char(64)`, lower-case.
- The family row and `guide.family_id` are also set by the migration, on DBs that had the brand.
- `url.ts`/`telegram.ts` restate their enum types (checked against the schema in their tests) so the Worker needs no schema.
- Out-of-Owns edits, type-only, forced by §2's new lesson kinds: `StudioBriefForm.tsx` and
  `bridge/lessons.ts` label maps; `bridge/clips.ts` filters; the old utm clips test updated.
