## Decisions

- The week on the home cards is UTC Monday–Sunday, same UTC days as `/posts`' date filter.
- "Make post" from a clip passes the summary (else title, else URL) as the topic; `/posts/new`
  has no clip parameter and adding one is S15's contract.
- `.prettierignore` stays unanchored: anchoring it reformats nine O10 files (promoted).
- Edits outside Owns (package.json, .env.example, .gitignore, docs, components of S14–S17)
  are the one-line mounts and hand-offs the lane 2 logs assigned to S20.
